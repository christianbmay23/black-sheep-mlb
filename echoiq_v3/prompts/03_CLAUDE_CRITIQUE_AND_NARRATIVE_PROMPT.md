Stress-test the card. Do not create a new card unless asked. Identify what EchoIQ may be missing.

1. Are official BETs too aggressive, too conservative, or properly gated?
2. Are any WATCHLIST plays wrongly excluded from the card?
3. Are any BETs actually just public-consensus noise?
4. Are any pitcher vulnerability scores too blunt?
5. Are weather/park factors over-weighted or under-weighted?
6. Are there lineup-cluster angles not captured by top-player ranking?
7. Are there hidden kill switches?

OUTPUT:

1. Agreement with EchoIQ official card
2. Disagreement / downgrade list
3. Upgrade candidates, if any
4. Biggest hidden risks
5. Best narrative game environments
6. Final critique in 5 bullets

HARD RULE:
Do not blend official bets, conditionals, watchlists, and external predictions. Keep categories separate.
